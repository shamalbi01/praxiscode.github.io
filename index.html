Stencil Notebook

A paper-like notebook for iPad and Apple Pencil. Write, sketch and keep every page in sync through your own iCloud.

Need help? Email info.praxiscode@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

My palm or finger leaves marks on the page

Turn on Settings → Draw with Apple Pencil only, or tap the pencil button at the top of a notebook. Your hand then won't draw, and a finger swipe turns pages.

How do I change the paper (lined, grid, dots)?

In a notebook, tap the page button at the top to change the current page. To add a page with a different paper, tap the add-page button and choose one.

My notebooks aren't on my other iPad

  • Check both iPads are signed in to the same Apple ID, and that iCloud is allowed for Stencil Notebook (iPadOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud).
  • In the app, open Settings and check that Sync with iCloud is on, then tap Sync now.
  • Large notebooks can take a few minutes over a slow connection.

I got a new iPad. How do I get my notes back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, install Stencil Notebook and open it. Your notebooks download from iCloud automatically.

How do I share or print a notebook?

Open the notebook, tap the … button and choose Export as PDF. You can then send, save or print it.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep the app free. A full-screen ad can appear when you open the app or come back to it after a while. Ads never appear while you write. To change how ads may use your data, open Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where privacy laws require it).
