Stencil Notebook Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Stencil Notebook is a handwriting notebook for iPad. Your notes and drawings are yours: they stay on your iPad and in your own iCloud account, and we never see them. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only part of the app that collects data.

Who we are

Stencil Notebook is published by Praxiscode Technologies Ltd, a company in Ireland. For this policy, we are the data controller. You can contact us about privacy at info.praxiscode@gmail.com.

Your notes and drawings

  • Notebooks, pages and drawings are stored on your iPad, in the app's folder (you can see it in the Files app).
  • If iCloud sync is on (it can be turned off in the app's Settings), they are also stored in your private iCloud database, provided by Apple. This lets them appear on your other iPads and come back if you get a new one. Only you can access this database; we can't read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • We don't have servers that receive your notes, and the app has no accounts or sign-in.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, it shows full-screen ads when you open it and when you return to it after a while. Ads never appear while you write. The ads come from Google AdMob, provided in the European Economic Area by Google Ireland Limited.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app collects:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking, see below) and identifiers specific to the app or to us.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address. Not your precise location.
  • Advertising data, such as which ads you've seen and tapped.
  • Product interaction, such as app launches.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data (for example, launch time).

Google does not receive your notes or drawings. How Google uses this information is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads may use your data for personalised advertising or store information on your device. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes one by one. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you refuse, you still see ads, but they aren't personalised.
  • Apple's tracking permission: the app asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier isn't shared and ads aren't based on your activity elsewhere. You can change this later in iPadOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising using the message shown in the app, or in Settings → Ad privacy choices. We don't sell personal information for money.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent for personalised advertising and for storing or reading information on your device for advertising. You can withdraw it at any time, as described above.
  • Legitimate interests for showing non-personalised ads that keep the app free, and for measuring ads and preventing fraud, where the consent message says a partner relies on it. You can object to this in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Performing our agreement with you for syncing your notes through your iCloud account when you turn sync on.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't use our own analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell your notes or drawings, or share them with anyone.

Apple

Apple provides the App Store, iCloud and, if you have agreed to share them with developers, crash reports and usage statistics. Apple processes that information under its own Privacy Policy.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. They rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long data is kept

Your notes are kept until you delete them, on your iPad and in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the copy on that iPad; to remove the iCloud copy, delete the notebooks in the app first, or remove the app's data in iPadOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. Google keeps advertising data for the periods set out in its privacy policy. We don't keep any personal data ourselves.

Children

Stencil Notebook isn't directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best made through the in-app ad choices or to Google and Apple directly, but we're happy to help: email info.praxiscode@gmail.com.

You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie, or to the authority where you live.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll also tell you in the app.
